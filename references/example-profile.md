**Provisos**

\* **Data freshness.** Excel rows reflect the UNCTAD ISDS Navigator full-data Excel, snapshot 31/12/2025 (1,463 treaty-based cases). The live layer comes from her ICSID profile (retrieved 2026-10-26; self-reported; page marked last updated 16 September 2024) and therefore captures **ICSID cases only** — any post-snapshot UNCITRAL, PCA, or SCC appointments are not visible from compliant free sources and nothing is asserted about them. The profile is a floor, not a case list; its silence on 2025–26 case numbers is not evidence of inactivity. Contract-based or annulment-phase cases on the profile fall outside UNCTAD's treaty-based scope and are excluded from the counts. Rows marked * combine the two sources and carry both limitations.

\*\* **Dissent counting.** An arbitrator is counted as dissenting only where a published individual opinion names them. Silence in a case with recorded opinions data is treated as no published opinion — it does not prove the decision was unanimous. The denominator (103) counts cases with at least one substantive decision (award, or decision on jurisdiction, merits/liability, or quantum) and opinions data recorded — consent awards, procedural orders, and discontinuances are excluded as not dissentable.

† **Outcomes are tribunal-level.** "Breach found" describes what the tribunal decided, not how this arbitrator voted; individual positions are confirmed only from the decisions and any separate opinions ("check the record on her votes").

‡ **Scope.** Treaty-based ISDS only; commercial-arbitration appointments and confidential cases are invisible; counts cannot be deemed exhaustive. Fuller analytics exist in subscription databases (Jus Mundi, GAR ART) not used by this tool. Not legal advice; research aid for counsel.
